Engineering

Since the start Fundi Kipusa has trained people in many areas of engineering such as fabrication, mechanics, robotics and vehicle engineering. At our workshop in Mtwapa we give people real training that helps them get jobs and encourages new ideas and solutions that are good for the environment. In our engineering workshop we. Build special vehicles, including tricycles and tuk tuks that are made to fit different business and community needs. One of our important achievements is the creation of a fully built solar-powered tuk tuk showing our support for clean energy, new ideas and technology made right here. Our group also focuses on tricycle geometry and chassis design making sure every vehicle is built to be stable strong, safe and able to carry weight. From the idea to the final test we make sure all our work meets high standards. We also offer repair and maintenance for all kinds of motor vehicles. We also fix engines giving good mechanical help,for both land and water transport.

Research & Development

Through our Engineering center Speed Shop Kenya We carry our research on the latest and modern Engineering technologies.

Education

We teach the youth on the latest Engineering technologies by practically involving them in building of our Ipots, repairing and modification of cars and building devices we use at our Hasiera farm.
